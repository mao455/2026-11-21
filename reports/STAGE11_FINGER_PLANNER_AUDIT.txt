ContactPlanner.plan accepts contact_counts=(2,), (3,) or (2,3), plus explicit finger_groups of discovered distal names. This is a planning input: it restricts the same bounded beam search, IK, individual position/normal error thresholds, caller safety callback and wrench/uncertainty tests. Unselected joint values remain equal to measured actual_q. Invalid groups and incompatible counts fail before the search. generic_local_proposal forwards these optional arguments.

Ranking now uses mean normalized position error, mean normalized normal error and mean geometry uncertainty per participating finger, plus URDF minimum command-travel time and support load scale. Every participating finger must pass its own error gate. The prior arbitrary cardinality penalty and summed residual/uncertainty terms no longer decide between counts. candidate_qualities exposes the score terms, fingers, requested groups/counts and final selected quality. Command travel is a lower bound under URDF velocity limits; it excludes acceleration and actuator settling. Generic wrench feasibility remains conditional on stated friction and effort assumptions, not measured forces.
